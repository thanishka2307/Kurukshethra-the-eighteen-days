Kurukshetra: The Eighteen Days

A cartoon-style game that brings the Mahabharata war to life.

The Mahabharata is an epic of duty, loyalty and difficult choices, and its war at Kurukshetra lasted eighteen days. This project turns that war into an interactive game: pick a warrior and live the war from their side

Features
18 days of war shown on a day-by-day tracker
6 playable warriors, three from each side
2 sides: the Pandavas and the Kauravas
Turn-based duels: Strike, Guard, unleash your special astra once it is charged, or take a foul blow that wins quickly but stains your dharma
Choices that matter: each decision at night in camp shifts three meters: Dharma, Honour and Loyalty, and the final ending reflects which you chose
Fated moments: Abhimanyu in the Chakravyuh, Karna's sinking wheel and the last day with Duryodhana keep the war true to the epic
How the game works
Choose a warrior. Pick one of six heroes, each with unique stats and a special ability.
Learn the story. Five short scenes explain how the war came, from Hastinapur to the sides being drawn.
Fight the key days. Play through six turning points, from Day 1 to the last day, Day 18.
Face the outcome. Your ending depends on the choices you made and the dharma you kept.
The warriors
Warrior	Side	Special ability
Arjuna	Pandava	Gandiva: Guard charges the astra twice as fast
Bhima	Pandava	Oath-rage: +3 attack when below half health
Abhimanyu	Pandava	Fearless: +2 damage, but fated to fall on Day 13
Bhishma	Kaurava	Chosen death: survives his first fatal blow
Karna	Kaurava	No kavacha: takes extra damage and is fated on Day 17
Duryodhana	Kaurava	Mace master: strikes hit +2 harder, fated on Day 18
